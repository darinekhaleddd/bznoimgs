Frequently Asked

Who can apply for an installment plan?

Students, employees and business owners who meet our simple requirements. Visit the showroom and we will guide you.

Can I pay the whole amount early?

Yes. Ask our team about early settlement when you sign your plan.

Do I need a guarantor?

It depends on the plan and the scooter model. Our team will confirm this when you apply.

Is maintenance available after I buy?

Of course. Our workshop at 5 Hassan Aflaton, Cairo, is open daily from 3:00 PM to 11:00 PM.
