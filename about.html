Our Story

More Than a Scooter Shop

Bikes Zone Scooters was created for riders in Cairo who want one trusted place for everything: new scooters, flexible payment, a skilled workshop, accessories and genuine parts.

From our showroom at 5 Hassan Aflaton, Cairo we welcome every customer with honest advice, fair prices and service that continues long after the sale.

  • Honest advice, no pressure
  • Easy installment plans
  • Professional workshop on site
  • Open daily from 3:00 PM to 11:00 PM
